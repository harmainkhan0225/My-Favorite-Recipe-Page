My Favorite Recipe: Chicken Biryani

Chicken Biryani is one of my favorite Pakistani foods. It is a delicious rice dish made with chicken, spices, and aromatic basmati rice. It is perfect for family meals and special occasions.

Chicken Biryani

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 grams chicken
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons biryani masala
  • Fresh coriander and mint
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Steps

  1. Wash the basmati rice and soak it for 20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and fry the sliced onions until golden.
  3. Add the chicken, tomatoes, yogurt, salt, and biryani masala.
  4. Cook the chicken until it becomes tender and the gravy thickens.
  5. Boil the soaked rice in water until it is partly cooked, then drain it.
  6. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture and add mint and coriander.
  7. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
  8. Mix gently and serve your hot chicken biryani.
